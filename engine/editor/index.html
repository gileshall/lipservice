The editor needs a mouse and a keyboard. It does not work by touch.
double click an empty spot to add a note
drag a note to move it; drag its right end to change its length
Enter, or click under a note, to type its syllable. Type several words and they fill the notes that follow
arrows move the selected notes; alt + left or right makes them shorter or longer
B breath before the note: always, never, or left to the singer
L vibrato point: slide to it, or jump to it
delete removes the selected notes or vibrato point
space sing, or stop
click the ruler to set where singing starts; while it sings, it jumps there. Home goes back to the start
drag along the ruler to mark a loop; drag its ends to adjust it
O loop on or off
K metronome click on or off
drag a number to change it (hold shift for small steps); click it to type; alt + click resets it
1 to 9 switch singer; M opens the mixer; G shows or hides the other singers' notes
double click a tab to rename its singer; the + tab adds a singer, and with shift held it copies this one
scroll to move along; pinch or ctrl + scroll zooms in time; alt + scroll zooms in pitch
